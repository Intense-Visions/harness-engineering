# ideate-fleet — fan out strategy-grounded ideation as the head of the fleet spine

**Status:** Draft · **Tier:** Large · **Type:** rigid skill (orchestrator)
**Family:** `-fleet` (the **ideate** stage — the head of the core conveyor, upstream of intake)
**Keywords:** fleet, orchestration, batch, ideation, strategy-grounding, ranked-shortlist, curation, anti-spam, fan-out, worktree, human-pick, batch-review

## Overview

The `-fleet` conveyor currently starts at **intake**: `issue-fleet` sorts a backlog that already exists. Nothing produces that backlog. Ideation is still a per-topic, human-driven act — `harness-ideate` converges **one** topic into one ranked artifact (evidence: `agents/skills/claude-code/harness-ideate/SKILL.md:22` — "exactly ONE ranked artifact per run"), and a team that wants candidates across five strategy tracks runs it five times by hand, then reconciles five separately-ranked lists into one decision itself. That reconciliation — dedup across topics, checking each candidate against what is already filed or already shipped, and cutting the union down to something a human can actually read — is exactly the attention tax the `-fleet` family exists to remove.

`ideate-fleet` fills that gap and becomes the **head of the spine**: **ideate → issue → adr → roadmap → pr**. It enumerates a queue of **themes** (strategy tracks and opportunity areas, grounded in `STRATEGY.md`), confirms the batch with the human once, fans out worktree-isolated subagents that each run the **real** `harness-ideate` pipeline for one theme to its ranked artifact, independently re-derives every ranking rather than trusting a subagent's report, and hands back **one curated ranked shortlist for a human to pick from**. Presenting that shortlist ends the run — what the human does with a pick is theirs.

The defining property is its terminal act, and it is a caution rather than a capability. **Ideation is low-precision by construction.** `harness-ideate` is asked for 5–25 candidates per topic whether or not that many good ones exist — its only defense is a stated preference for fewer-but-distinct over padded near-duplicates, and nothing stops a merely-plausible idea from filling a slot; across six themes that is a hundred-plus ideas, most of which should never become work. A fleet that auto-filed them would convert one human's afternoon of thinking into a hundred tracking issues that `issue-fleet` then has to triage — the backlog-spam failure mode, transposed one stage upstream and amplified by the fan-out. So `ideate-fleet` **files nothing**. Its product is a curated, deduped, bounded shortlist; only ideas a human explicitly picks become issues or roadmap rows, and those enter the conveyor at intake like any other. This is `bug-fleet`'s anti-spam discipline (evidence: `agents/skills/claude-code/bug-fleet/SKILL.md:38` — no reproduction, no filing) applied at the stage where the precision is lowest and the temptation to over-produce is highest.

The member advances the **Upstream grounding** track (`STRATEGY.md#tracks` — "make the strategic and knowledge substrate … durable enough that downstream skills ground reliably instead of starting cold") by making `STRATEGY.md` the queue itself rather than one more optional input, and the **Full-lifecycle reach** track by extending the fleet family past the build loop to the intent-authoring edge.

### Goals

- Turn a batch of strategy-grounded themes into **one curated ranked shortlist** with a single up-front human touchpoint. The run ends when the shortlist is presented; the pick that follows is a human act outside the run.
- Dogfood the real per-theme pipeline (`harness-ideate`) — never hand-generate ideas — and prove it ran by the artifact it necessarily leaves behind.
- Hold an anti-spam bar at the stage with the least precision: dedup across themes, cross-check novelty against what is already tracked or shipped, and cut to a bounded shortlist rather than dumping the union.
- Make ranking auditable by **re-deriving** it — the orchestrator recomputes every score from the artifact's own recorded inputs instead of accepting the subagent's order.
- **File nothing.** No issue, no roadmap row, no spec, no PR leaves this fleet.

### Non-goals (YAGNI)

- Filing issues or roadmap rows for generated ideas, in any quantity, under any confidence.
- Writing specs, plans, ADRs, or code — `harness-ideate`'s Iron Law forbids it per run, and the fleet inherits that boundary for the batch.
- Reimplementing idea generation, critique, or the `(impact × confidence) ÷ effort` ranking math — the fleet composes `harness-ideate` and re-derives its scores; it does not fork the scoring contract (evidence: `agents/skills/claude-code/harness-ideate/references/scoring.md`).
- Writing or repairing `STRATEGY.md` — that is `harness-strategy`; this member reads it, exactly as `harness-ideate` does.
- Ranking the existing roadmap — that is `harness-roadmap-pilot`; this member generates fresh candidates.
- Triaging, deduping, or routing an existing issue backlog — that is `issue-fleet`, immediately downstream.
- Building a physical shared `-fleet` library — the shared contract stays documented in `docs/reference/fleet-family.md`.

## Decisions made

1. **Build on the documented family spine; define only the stage-specific parts.** `ideate-fleet` reuses the shared five-phase skeleton (SELECT → CONFIRM → DISPATCH → VERIFY → terminal), the concurrency governor, the artifact-based verification discipline, the worktree fan-out with its nested-path push caveat, the one-up-front-CONFIRM / park-unforeseen interaction model, and the never-file-unreviewed-work invariant — all stated once in `docs/reference/fleet-family.md`. The `SKILL.md` cites that page rather than restating it, and defines only what is this member's own: its theme queue, its curation taxonomy, its artifact-collection step, its re-derived-ranking verification, its shortlist terminal act, and its domain-specific rationalizations.

2. **Queue: disjoint themes, sourced from `STRATEGY.md` tracks and human-supplied opportunity areas.** A **theme** is one coherent ideation topic — the unit that becomes one `harness-ideate` run, in one worktree. SELECT composes two sources: the `Tracks` section of `STRATEGY.md` (via `read_strategy`, evidence: `agents/skills/claude-code/harness-ideate/SKILL.md:39`) and any opportunity areas the human supplied via `--themes`. Themes are required to be **disjoint** — two themes whose focus lines overlap produce the same idea twice in two worktrees, which is how a fleet becomes a duplicate factory — and the batch is **bounded** (default 6 themes). Disjointness is not merely asserted: SELECT compares every pair of candidate focus lines and, where two overlap, **merges them into one theme with a combined focus line** (or, if the human prefers, splits them along the stated boundary at CONFIRM); every merge is shown in the CONFIRM round with both source lines, so the human sees what was folded together before anything fans out. Rationale: making tracks the queue is what makes the fleet strategy-grounded by construction rather than by reminder; the alternative of deriving themes from the codebase was rejected as inventing strategy the human never committed to. **If `STRATEGY.md` is absent or invalid and no `--themes` were supplied, the fleet stops and reports — there is nothing to fan out over.** A present-but-invalid `STRATEGY.md` surfaces its error verbatim and degrades to supplied themes; the fleet never repairs it. When `read_strategy` itself is unreachable (no harness MCP server), SELECT uses `harness-ideate`'s documented `@harness-engineering/core` fallback; if that is also unavailable the run is treated as _no strategy source_ — supplied themes proceed ungrounded, and with none the fleet stops and reports.

3. **Per-theme pipeline: the real `harness-ideate`, run to its ranked artifact, with its interactive prompts front-loaded at CONFIRM.** `harness-ideate` has two interactive stops — an inputs confirmation and a post-critique "which objections do you want to answer" selection (evidence: `agents/skills/claude-code/harness-ideate/SKILL.md:56` and `:101-109`). A fan-out cannot pause per theme, so both are answered from CONFIRM-time policy rather than skipped: the inputs are the confirmed theme focus line and candidate count, and the **objection policy defaults to `none`** — every strongest objection stands as an accepted downside. Rationale: an unanswered objection lowers an idea's standing, which is the honest default for machine-generated rebuttals; a fleet that auto-answered its own critiques would inflate its own rankings, and the human who picks from the shortlist reads the standing objection as part of the pick. The subagent **runs the real skill to its artifact and never hand-generates or hand-edits candidates** — the artifact under `docs/ideation/` (resolved as Decision 6 states) is what VERIFY checks for, exactly as a plan directory is for `roadmap-fleet`.

4. **Iron Law — NOTHING IS FILED; ideas leave only as a shortlist a human picks from.** The fleet never creates an issue, never adds or mutates a roadmap row, never writes a spec, plan, or ADR, and never opens a PR. Rationale: ideation is the lowest-precision stage in the lifecycle — `harness-ideate` fills its requested count with whatever is plausible, and the fan-out multiplies that by the theme count. Auto-filing would hand `issue-fleet` a backlog the fleet itself manufactured, making the intake stage's job harder rather than easier, and would launder machine speculation into tracked work. `bug-fleet` refuses to file a defect without a reproduction because confident prose is not evidence; the same reasoning applies with more force here, because an idea has no equivalent of a failing test. The human pick is the only gate that converts an idea into work.

5. **Curation is a bounded cut with three mechanical filters, not a ranked dump.** Presenting the union of every theme's candidates would be the spam failure mode by another route — a hundred-row table is not a shortlist. Three filters run, in order, and each records what it dropped:
   - **Novelty cross-check (per theme, in DISPATCH).** Each preselected candidate is checked against open issues, existing roadmap rows, and **recently-shipped features — PRs merged within a confirmed lookback window (default 90 days) plus roadmap rows in a done state**. An already-tracked or already-shipped idea is annotated **already-known** and dropped **citing the issue, row, or PR that covers it** — never re-surfaced as new. A drop **backfills** from the next-highest below-cut candidate in that theme until the per-theme cut is refilled or the theme's candidates are exhausted, and each backfill is recorded. If a cross-check source is unavailable (`gh` unauthenticated, no tracker, no roadmap), the candidate is annotated **novelty-unknown**, not `novel`: it stays eligible, the shortlist row says the check could not run and names the missing source, and the batch's assumptions note records it — the fleet never launders an unrun check into a novelty claim.
   - **Cross-theme dedup (in CURATE).** Themes are disjoint by construction, so this is a backstop. Two candidates with the same premise collapse into one entry citing **both** themes; a premise differing only by a parameter is one idea, per `harness-ideate`'s own near-duplicate rule (evidence: `agents/skills/claude-code/harness-ideate/SKILL.md:77`).
   - **The bounded cut.** Default **top 3 per theme**, global shortlist cap **10**, both confirmed at CONFIRM. The two bounds routinely collide (6 themes × 3 = 18 > 10), so the cap is resolved by a stated rule rather than by whoever finishes first: **one slot is reserved for the highest-scoring survivor of every non-thin theme** (so no theme is silently erased from the shortlist), then the remaining slots are filled by **re-derived final score descending across all themes**, ties broken by the theme's SELECT order and then by artifact order. If the reserved slots alone exceed the cap, the cap is raised to the theme count and the raise is reported. Everything below the cut stays in its per-theme artifact, which the shortlist links — nothing is destroyed, only un-promoted.

   Rationale: the fleet's value is a human reading one page and picking, and every filter is recorded so the drops are spot-checkable rather than silent.

6. **Verification: artifact provenance plus an independently re-derived ranking — never a self-report, and CI is not applicable.** The family invariant requires proof the real per-item pipeline ran and forbids accepting a subagent's word for it. This member produces **no code and no PR**, so the family's all-OS-CI half has no subject — it is recorded as **not applicable** rather than quietly dropped, and its evidentiary weight is carried by a second check instead. VERIFY therefore runs two independent checks per theme:
   - **Provenance** — the ranked artifact exists under `docs/ideation/` with frontmatter `topic` matching the theme's confirmed focus line. The artifact is **resolved by that frontmatter, not by an exact filename**: `harness-ideate` truncates slugs to 30 characters, and the collector applies its hex-suffix collision rule on arrival (Decision 9), which the retry path below and two themes sharing a truncated slug both trigger — an exact-path check would reject its own pipeline's legitimate output. It must carry the frontmatter `harness-ideate` mandates (`topic`, `generated_at`, `strategy_grounded`, `strategy_path`, `count_requested`, `count_generated`, `ranking_formula`), with `count_requested` equal to the confirmed candidate count and `count_generated` equal to the number of candidates actually present. Each candidate carries the six fields the artifact persists (premise, persona, complexity, impact, confidence, effort — `key_risk` is the seed the artifact renders as the objection paragraph, not a separate field) plus a strongest-objection paragraph. Absent or malformed ⇒ the pipeline did not run ⇒ **rejected**, however good the ideas look. A `count_generated` **below** `count_requested` is **not** a rejection: `harness-ideate` is required to prefer fewer-but-distinct over padded near-duplicates, so a documented shortfall is reported as **thin** — only a count that exceeds the confirmed request, or candidates missing their fields, indicates the contract was not honored.
   - **Re-derived ranking** — the orchestrator recomputes `(impact × confidence) ÷ effort` from each candidate's own recorded impact/confidence/effort using the published `1|2|3` mapping, and confirms that **each candidate's recorded base score equals the recomputed one** and that the artifact's order is **non-increasing in final score**. Order is checked as a monotonicity property, not as a single permitted permutation: the `1|2|3` mapping yields only nine distinct base-score values, so exact ties are the common case, and `harness-ideate` breaks them by generation order — which the artifact does not persist. Demanding one exact order would reject legitimate artifacts for an unknowable reason. It further confirms any strategy-alignment bonus stayed inside its bounds (max `+0.75`, applied only when `|Δbase_score| ≤ 0.05`) and that a bonus outside a tie window did not reorder anything. A recomputed-score mismatch, a non-monotonic order, or an out-of-bounds bonus is **rejected**, not corrected — an order that does not follow from the recorded inputs means the ranking was asserted rather than computed, and re-deriving is the only check an eloquent subagent cannot talk its way past.

   A rejected theme is **retried once**; still failing, it is reported as rejected and the batch continues.

7. **Terminal act: one curated shortlist document; the run ends at its presentation.** CURATE-AND-REPORT writes a single batch shortlist to `docs/ideation/shortlists/<batch-slug>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` — a distinct namespace from the per-theme artifacts, so nothing the fleet writes can be mistaken for a `harness-ideate` run's own output or violate its one-artifact-per-run law. Each row carries the premise, its theme, the re-derived score, the standing strongest objection, the novelty citation or `novelty-unknown` note, and a link to its per-theme artifact (collected per Decision 9, so the link resolves), plus the batch's assumptions-made note. The fleet then presents the shortlist for the human to pick from and **stops** — presentation is the end of the run, and no fleet action is defined for what follows. Picks are routed by hand to `harness-brainstorming` (to spec one) or to the roadmap (to enqueue several); the fleet performs neither, and does not wait for them. Rationale: the routing act is the filing act, and Decision 4 puts it on the human's side of the line.

8. **A thin theme is a valid, valuable result.** A theme is **thin** when, after the novelty cross-check, backfill, and cross-theme dedup, **no candidate survives to the shortlist** — every candidate was already-known or collapsed into another theme's entry — or when its artifact verified with `count_generated` below the confirmed request. Thin is a reported outcome, not a failed run. Rationale: the pressure to return something so a sweep does not look wasted is precisely what produces the shortlist nobody trusts. A theme reported thin tells the human that track is well-covered, which is information; padding it destroys the property that makes the shortlist worth reading at all. Note what thin is _not_: a low-scoring candidate is not filtered out for being unimpressive — this member has no quality floor beyond the bounded cut, because a score threshold on machine-generated estimates would be a second, unfounded judgment layered on the first.

9. **Artifacts come home; nothing is committed.** Each subagent's `harness-ideate` run writes its artifact inside that theme's worktree, and this member pushes nothing — so without a collection step the artifacts die with the worktrees and every shortlist link dangles. Before its worktree is released, each theme's artifact is therefore **copied verbatim** into the invoking working tree's `docs/ideation/`, and the shortlist links those copies. Copying is not editing: the file lands byte-identical, and Decision 4's bar is unaffected because writing a local file is not filing — no issue, roadmap row, spec, or PR is created, and **the fleet does not commit, stage, or push anything**; the collected artifacts and the shortlist are working-tree changes the human keeps or discards. Filename collisions are resolved **at collection, not in the worktree**: each worktree starts with its own empty `docs/ideation/`, so a subagent never observes a collision and never applies `harness-ideate`'s hex-suffix rule — two themes whose focus lines truncate to the same 30-character slug, and a retry re-running the same focus on the pinned date, would otherwise arrive as the same filename and one would silently overwrite the other. The collector therefore applies that same rule on arrival, deriving the 6-character suffix from `sha1(focus + generated_at)` exactly as `harness-ideate` specifies, so both files coexist and each stays resolvable by its frontmatter `topic` and `generated_at`. A theme whose artifact cannot be collected (destination unwritable, worktree unreadable) is **parked** with the filesystem error surfaced verbatim, not rejected — a collection failure is the orchestrator's problem, and calling it a failed pipeline run would blame the subagent for the fleet's own plumbing. The **batch date** used for both the shortlist filename and artifact resolution is **pinned in UTC at CONFIRM** and reused for the whole batch, so a run spanning UTC midnight does not scatter its artifacts across two dates — the same reason `bug-fleet` pins one base SHA per batch.

## Technical design

### Skill shape

A claude-code rigid skill at `agents/skills/claude-code/ideate-fleet/` (`SKILL.md` plus `skill.yaml`), orchestrator-tier (`tier: 2`, `cognitive_mode: systematic-orchestrator`), with a domain-specific `## Rationalizations to Reject`. Platform variants (codex, cursor, gemini-cli, antigravity) are generated. The skill body carries no internal roadmap/PR/issue numbers — the family spine and ADRs are cited by title only, so the shipped skill reads correctly in any adopter project.

### The loop — five phases (stage-specific detail only; the skeleton is the family spine)

1. **SELECT.** Read `STRATEGY.md` via `read_strategy` (falling back as Decision 2 states); fold its `Tracks` bullets and any `--themes` opportunity areas into themes within the batch bound (default 6), **merging any pair whose focus lines overlap** so the batch is disjoint by construction. Score and order by strategic weight — track membership, whether the theme touches the `Target problem` or `Our approach`, and how thinly the theme is already covered by existing roadmap rows — reusing `harness-roadmap-pilot`-style impact scoring so the order is principled and reproducible. No theme source at all ⇒ stop and report.
2. **CONFIRM.** One up-front human round: the ranked themes with their strategic basis and **any merges made for disjointness, showing both source lines**; the per-theme candidate count (default 10, clamped to `harness-ideate`'s `[5, 25]`); the **objection policy** (default `none`); the **per-theme shortlist cut** (default 3) and **global shortlist cap** (default 10, resolved by the reserved-slot rule of Decision 5); the **novelty lookback window** (default 90 days); the **pinned UTC batch date**; the proposed concurrency (default 2, max ~3); and the explicit restatement that **nothing will be filed and nothing committed**. The human approves or trims once. Under `--dry-run` the skill stops here.
3. **DISPATCH.** One worktree-isolated subagent per confirmed theme, each running **GROUND → GENERATE → CRITIQUE → RANK → WRITE** — the real `harness-ideate` pipeline, with the interactive stops answered from CONFIRM policy — then **PRESELECT** its top-K, **CROSS-CHECK** each preselected candidate for novelty (backfilling below-cut candidates for already-known drops), and **COLLECT** the artifact verbatim into the invoking tree's `docs/ideation/` before the worktree is released. The per-theme artifact is **never edited** by the fleet; cross-check results live in the returned record. Capped at the governor. An unforeseen fork parks that one theme and reports it.
4. **VERIFY.** Independently, never by self-report: resolve the collected artifact by its frontmatter `topic`, confirm the mandated frontmatter, the count semantics, and complete per-candidate fields; recompute every base score from the artifact's own inputs and confirm score equality, non-increasing final-score order, and the bounded tiebreaker. Verdicts: `verified`, `thin`, `parked`, `rejected` (retried once first; where a focus has two collected artifacts, the one with the later `generated_at` — the retry's — is the one verified). All-OS CI is **not applicable** and is recorded as such.
5. **CURATE-AND-REPORT.** Run the cross-theme dedup backstop, apply the bounded cut with its reserved-slot rule, write the single curated shortlist to `docs/ideation/shortlists/<batch-slug>-<pinned-UTC-date>.md`, and present it for the human to pick from. Report counts and reasons for every non-shortlisted outcome: already-known drops with citations, `novelty-unknown` annotations with the missing source, backfills applied, cross-theme dedup collapses, below-the-cut counts, thin themes, parked themes, and rejected themes. **Nothing is filed and nothing is committed.**

### Key seams and data

- **`Theme`** record: sources (`strategy-track` | `supplied`), id/slug, focus line, any merged-from focus lines, strategic basis, composite score, detected forks.
- **`Candidate`** record: theme, premise, persona, complexity, impact/confidence/effort, recorded base score, re-derived base score, alignment bonus applied (`yes`/`no` + reason), standing strongest objection, novelty (`novel` | `already-known` + citation | `novelty-unknown` + missing source), shortlist verdict (`shortlisted` | `backfilled-shortlisted` | `below-cut` | `deduped-into` + target | `already-known`).
- **`Batch`** record: theme list, pinned UTC batch date, confirmed count/cut/cap/lookback/governor and objection policy, per-theme verdicts, collected artifact paths.
- **Reuses:** `read_strategy` for grounding; the real `harness-ideate` per theme; `harness-roadmap-pilot`-style scoring for theme ordering; `harness-strategy` as the read-only boundary; the subagent worktree-isolation primitive for fan-out; `gh` and the roadmap for the novelty cross-check only.
- **Arguments:** `--themes` (opportunity areas folded into the queue), `--count`, `--cut`, `--cap`, `--concurrency`, `--lookback`, `--dry-run` (stop after CONFIRM). Every one has a default and is restated in the CONFIRM round, so a bare invocation is a complete invocation.
- **Concurrency governor** at 2 (default), max ~3 — the shared machine-storm limit.

### Assumptions

- The harness MCP server is reachable for `read_strategy`; if not, `harness-ideate`'s `@harness-engineering/core` fallback is used, and failing that the run is ungrounded or stops (Decision 2).
- `gh` is authenticated against a GitHub-hosted tracker and a roadmap is present; when either is missing the novelty cross-check degrades to `novelty-unknown` rather than to a novelty claim (Decision 5).
- The subagent worktree-isolation primitive from the family spine is available, and worktrees are created outside a `.claude/`-nested path per the spine's push-path caveat.
- `docs/ideation/` is a tracked (not ignored) path, so collected artifacts and the shortlist appear as ordinary working-tree changes; the fleet leaves them uncommitted and the human decides whether they land.
- Dates are UTC throughout, matching `harness-ideate`'s own date rule; the batch date is pinned once at CONFIRM.

### Integration Points

- **Entry Points** — A new claude-code skill `ideate-fleet` (`harness skill run ideate-fleet`) and its MCP `run_skill` mapping. No new CLI command, MCP tool, or route.
- **Registrations Required** — Skill discovery regeneration: `harness generate` regenerates the plugin command directories, `docs/reference/skills-catalog.md`, and the platform variants (codex, cursor, gemini-cli, antigravity). A roadmap shard `docs/roadmap.d/ideate-fleet.md` registers the member in the family milestone, and the `docs/roadmap.md` aggregate is regenerated from it.
- **Documentation Updates** — Add `ideate-fleet`'s row to the Members table in `docs/reference/fleet-family.md` and place it at the head of that page's conveyor sentence. Add the roadmap shard described above. No other doc edits beyond the generated catalog.
- **Architectural Decisions** — None new. `ideate-fleet` consumes the already-ratified family ADRs by title: _Subagent worktree fan-out (vs the Workflow primitive) for -fleet execution_ and _The front-load / park-unforeseen interaction model for the -fleet family_. The nothing-is-filed bar (Decision 4) is a member-local discipline expressed as the skill's Iron Law and Gates — it constrains this member's terminal act only, not the family contract — so it warrants no standalone ADR. The same applies to artifact collection (Decision 9): it is the plumbing a push-nothing member needs to get its subagents' evidence out of disposable worktrees, not a change to the fan-out ADR it implements.
- **Knowledge Impact** — Extends the `-fleet` family concept in the graph with its head stage, and with a new verification shape: a member whose evidence is an artifact plus an **independently re-derived score**, because it emits no code and therefore has no CI signal to lean on — and whose evidence must be **collected out of its worktrees rather than pushed**, since it opens no PR to carry it.

## Success Criteria

- Given a confirmed batch of N themes, the fleet produces **exactly one** curated ranked shortlist at `docs/ideation/shortlists/<batch-slug>-<pinned-UTC-date>.md`, and **no issue, roadmap row, spec, plan, ADR, or PR is created**.
- The fleet **commits, stages, and pushes nothing**: the shortlist and the collected per-theme artifacts are left as working-tree changes.
- **Every shortlisted candidate traces to a verified per-theme artifact** collected into `docs/ideation/`, resolved by its frontmatter `topic` rather than by an exact filename, so `harness-ideate`'s 30-character slug truncation and hex-suffix collision rule do not cause false rejections; a theme with no artifact, or a malformed one, is rejected as not having run the real pipeline.
- **Every shortlisted candidate's score is independently re-derived** by the orchestrator from the artifact's own impact/confidence/effort values; a candidate whose recorded base score differs from the recomputed one, or an artifact whose final-score order is not non-increasing, is rejected rather than silently re-sorted. Exact score ties in any order are accepted, because the generation order that breaks them is not persisted.
- A verified artifact whose `count_generated` is **below** the confirmed request is reported as **thin**, not rejected; only a count exceeding the request, or candidates missing their persisted fields (premise, persona, complexity, impact, confidence, effort, strongest objection), is a rejection.
- The strategy-alignment bonus is confirmed to have stayed within its published bounds (max `+0.75`, applied only when `|Δbase_score| ≤ 0.05`) and to have reordered nothing outside a tie window; an out-of-bounds application is a rejection.
- The shortlist is **bounded** — no more than the confirmed per-theme cut and global cap — and when the two collide the cap is resolved by the reserved-slot rule (one slot per non-thin theme, remaining slots by re-derived score), so no non-thin theme is silently erased. Every below-the-cut candidate remains reachable through its linked, collected per-theme artifact.
- Already-tracked or already-shipped ideas — open issues, roadmap rows, and PRs merged within the confirmed lookback window (default 90 days) — are **dropped citing the covering issue, roadmap row, or PR**, never re-surfaced as new, and each drop **backfills** from the next below-cut candidate until the cut refills or the theme is exhausted.
- When a novelty source is unavailable, affected candidates are annotated **`novelty-unknown`** naming the missing source — never reported as `novel`.
- Themes are **disjoint on dispatch**: any overlapping pair found in SELECT is merged (or split) before CONFIRM, and every merge is shown to the human with both source focus lines.
- Cross-theme duplicates are **collapsed into one entry citing both themes**, with the collapse recorded.
- Every shortlist entry carries its **standing strongest objection**, and the batch carries an **"assumptions made"** note (theme derivation basis and any merges, pinned batch date, objection policy, cut and cap applied, novelty calls including unavailable sources).
- A theme with **no candidate surviving** the novelty cross-check, backfill, and dedup — or one whose artifact generated fewer candidates than requested — is reported as **thin**: a valid outcome, not a failure. No score threshold filters candidates beyond the bounded cut.
- There is **exactly one** up-front human decision round, and the run ends at shortlist presentation; no per-theme interactive pauses except a genuinely-unforeseen fork parked to its own theme, and no fleet action follows a human pick.
- When `STRATEGY.md` is absent or invalid **and** no themes were supplied, the fleet **stops and reports** rather than inventing a queue; when it is invalid but themes were supplied, the validation error is surfaced verbatim and the batch proceeds ungrounded — observable as `strategy_grounded: false` in every per-theme artifact and recorded in the batch's assumptions note. The same applies when `read_strategy` and its `@harness-engineering/core` fallback are both unreachable.
- The fleet **never modifies `STRATEGY.md`** and **never edits a per-theme `harness-ideate` artifact** — collected copies are byte-identical to what the subagent's run wrote.
- No theme is marked verified on a subagent self-report — every verdict is backed by an independently-read artifact and an independently-recomputed ranking.
- Concurrency never exceeds the confirmed governor (default 2, max ~3), and no theme exceeds the confirmed candidate count.
